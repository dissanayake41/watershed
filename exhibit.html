This view uses our terrain, river and reservoir model, including Mannar Island and 136 selected major water bodies. Natural colours and sea shading are illustrative, not satellite imagery or measured bathymetry.

The highlight uses the unexaggerated SRTM elevation data: only land above 300 metres is selected. “Only >300 m” displays the separate upper part of the print model. Heights are enlarged ten times for visibility; this does not change the 300-metre threshold. Screen meshes are simplified and are not printing files.

Water-body sources: project mapping (2018) and © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). This is an exhibition model, not a navigation map.
