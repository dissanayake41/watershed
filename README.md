# Upper Watershed Management — V27 hotspot dashboard

Live exhibition: https://dissanayake41.github.io/watershed/
ADB dashboard: https://dissanayake41.github.io/watershed/#adb-dashboard

The main menu has five photographic options: Upper Mahaweli, Knuckles, Upper Watershed Restoration (ADB dashboard), Sri Lanka in 3D, and Riverkeeper. The complete ADB presentation remains accessible through Detailed assessment in the dashboard and through its existing #adb link.

## Dashboard

1. Basin priorities retains the eight-basin rapid-screening map and linked ranking. Sequential pale aqua, teal and dark blue colours distinguish relative priority.
2. Uma Oya findings shows actual shared hotspot locations from the final 30 m classified raster, rather than colouring whole sub-basins. Orange marks cells shared by the highest 20% soil-loss and sediment-delivery groups. Pale green is other analysed land; grey is excluded area. Touch identifies a location. Explore opens zoom, pan, pinch and reset controls. The 5 km scale is shown at the initial zoom. The separate contribution chart, seasonal flow and field-sampling results remain.
3. Restoration outlook retains climate projections and the separate conservation comparison.

Source: Hotspots_top20_comparison.tif and Uma_Oya_boundary in Uma_Oya_Priority_Zones.gpkg from the final InVEST spatial-priority outputs. Cell size is 30 m; coordinate system EPSG:5235. The display re-colours classes without changing their locations or interpolating values. Shared hotspot code is 4. Pixel counts are not substituted for the area-weighted statistics in the report. Report values: 123.08 km² shared hotspots, 17.3% of analysed land, 68.9% of soil loss and 77.8% of sediment delivery. Original source reports and research folders remain local.

Primary reports: Rapid Assessment (8 May 2026), Eco-Hydrological Assessment (August 2026), Final Report (26 October 2026). Sources & scope identifies tables and limitations. Water-quality observations, modelled historical flows, future climate and conservation scenarios remain distinct. Modelled benefits do not establish completed interventions or measured restoration impacts.

## Menu motion

Larger particles and subtle moving arcs orbit the photo options. Motion runs while the menu is open and pauses for detailed content, a hidden page, the visitor's Pause control or reduced-motion preferences. Background-video failure no longer disables these independent animations. The approved welcome scene and movie are unchanged.

## Local use

Open OPEN_Upper_Watershed_Management_V27.html for the complete portable offline exhibition. All 94 media assets are embedded. The Web ZIP contains the static site; serve it over HTTP or publish through GitHub Pages. There are no external runtime dependencies or build steps.

V26 remains in its separate version folder. Local qa/ holds map provenance, browser-check results and screenshots; these are not part of the public site.
